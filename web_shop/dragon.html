Info

The ender dragon is a flying hostile boss mob
that spawns on the central island of the End when the player first enters the dimension.
It is the largest naturally spawning mob in the game.
Its attacks include charging at the player and launching fireballs,
which create damaging area effect clouds.
Its initial defeat drops 12000XP experience.

A leprechaun is a diminutive supernatural being in Irish folklore,
classed by some as a type of solitary fairy. They are usually depicted as little bearded men,
wearing a coat and hat, who partake in mischief.
In later times, they have been depicted as shoe-makers who have a hidden pot of gold at the end of the rainbow.
